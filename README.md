# Real Estate Market Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes apartment listings in Saint Petersburg and the surrounding area to identify the key factors associated with property prices.

The analysis focuses on understanding how apartment characteristics, location and listing information relate to the final market price.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A real estate platform wants to better understand the local housing market and identify the characteristics that have the greatest impact on apartment prices.

The key questions are:

* Which apartment characteristics are most strongly associated with price?
* How does location affect property value?
* Does the floor of an apartment influence its price?
* Has the real estate market changed over time?
* Does the publication date affect the listing price?

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains information about apartment listings collected from the Yandex Real Estate service.

Key features include:

* Apartment area
* Number of rooms
* Location
* Floor
* Total number of floors in the building
* Listing price
* Publication date
* Other property characteristics

---

## 🔎 Analysis Workflow

The project includes:

1. Initial data exploration
2. Missing-value analysis and preprocessing
3. Outlier detection and treatment
4. Feature engineering
5. Price-per-square-meter calculation
6. Analysis of publication dates
7. Exploratory data analysis
8. Identification of the main factors associated with apartment prices

---

## 💡 Key Findings

The analysis showed that apartment price is strongly associated with:

* **Total apartment area**
* **Location**
* **Floor**

Apartments located on the first and top floors tend to have lower prices compared with apartments on other floors.

The exact day and month of publication did not show a meaningful relationship with price. However, apartment prices increased over the years covered by the dataset, indicating long-term growth in the local real estate market.

---

## 📈 Business Insights

The results can help real estate platforms and analysts:

* estimate typical property prices;
* identify important pricing factors;
* segment the real estate market by location and property characteristics;
* better understand long-term market dynamics.

For a production pricing model, the next step would be to build a predictive model using the features identified during the exploratory analysis.
